# Level Eligibility{id=placementeligibility}

All levels must be legitimately verified by a single player, including solo verifications of 2-player levels. Levels will only be placed if proof of verification is provided to the list team, and this proof must meet our current standards for record eligibility.

Levels without verification proof can also be placed once completed by another player, regardless of verification rights.

## Rated Placements{id=ratedplacements}
In order to be placed on the Demon List, a level must be **rated in-game** and must be considered *harder than the current demon placed at #150*.

## Unrated Placements{id=unratedplacements}
### Eligibility Criteria{id=eligibilitycriteria}
In order to be placed on the Rated+ List, an unrated level must meet the following criteria:
- Must be harder than the current level placed at #150.
- Cannot be shorter than 30 seconds.
- Cannot be a layout which does not contain effects or does not follow the layout style for decorative/thematic purposes.
- Cannot be slightly modified variants of existing levels without drastically altered decoration, such as but not limited to:
  - Buffed
  - Nerfed
  - Rebalanced
  - Unnerfed
  - Former verifier version
  - Repeated multiple times in a row
  - Merged with another level
  - Invisible gameplay
  - Recoloured
- A level which fails to meet eligibility criteria may still be polled based on historical significance, entirely at the Demon List team's discretion.
### Voting System{id=votingsystem}
Unrated levels that meet the eligibility criteria are placed in a voting pool in an arbitrary order by the list team. Polls are periodically created for an **independent voting committee**, consisting of qualified applicants including former and current in-game moderators and rating advisors, to vote for or against each level's addition to the list. These votes are plugged into a formula that converts votes into an approval percentage, resulting in a sorted list of levels ordered from highest to lowest approval rating. 

A level **must have the highest approval rating out of all unplaced levels** before it can be placed. A formula is used to determine rate of placement so that list team members do not control whether a specific level is added.
### Voting Standards{id=votingstandards}
- Voters should aim for the average player to view the levels placed on the Rated+ List as clear, justifiable choices based on a level's gameplay and decoration quality, and allow the **general quality thresholds of the rate system** to directly inform and guide voting decisions. 
- A level's historical significance to the game should influence votes.
- The usage of songs not on Newgrounds should not influence votes.
- The status of creators/verifiers involved in the level should not influence votes in any meaningful way. However, individuals involved in serious controversy may have their names removed/censored from a level's credits at the staff team's discretion as to explicitly condemn their actions and limit attention given to them.
- Above all else, voters are expected to be consistent in their standards.
