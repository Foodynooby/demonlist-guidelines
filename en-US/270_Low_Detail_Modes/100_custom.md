# Custom LDMs{id=custom-ldms}

Players are _strongly advised_ to clear any custom LDMs with the team before using them, especially if an LDM for the level already exists. Strong level LDMs may be considered nerfs, which violates our guidelines for [gameplay changes](/guidelines/eligibility#gameplay).

**Using a custom LDM carries the risk of a rejected record**! Even if an LDM has been approved in the past, it is always recommended to ask the list team whether it is still acceptable. Players may refer to our [Copy Tracker](https://arkane.moe/copy-tracker/) for a list of approved LDMs.

## Custom LDM Guidelines{id=custom-ldm-guidelines}

A custom LDM may not _delete or otherwise alter any object that obstructs the player’s vision_. All gameplay must be kept exactly the same as the original level.

No part of the LDM should look visually "empty" compared to the original level.

The length of the LDM should be the exact same as the original level.

Remember that you can submit a custom LDM for its approval and subsequent addition to the LDMs Database to any list member!

## Player Altered Decoration{id=player-altered-decoration}

Records with any player-altered decoration (eg. altered text or added art) are generally not eligible for the list. Some edits may be given exceptions if insignificant enough, but only with **explicit approval** from a member of the list team. Visual edits present while gameplay is on-screen will **never** be acceptable!
